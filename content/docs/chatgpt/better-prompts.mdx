---
title: Give useful instructions
description: Build a clear prompt from the few pieces of context that genuinely affect the result.
---

A good prompt is not necessarily long. It contains the information that keeps
ChatGPT from making an important wrong assumption.

## Six useful pieces

1. **Goal** — what should be achieved?
2. **Context** — what situation does the task belong to?
3. **Source material** — which facts, notes, or text should be used?
4. **Audience** — who will read or use the result?
5. **Format** — what should the answer look like?
6. **Quality criteria** — how will you recognize a good result?

You do not need all six every time. Include the pieces that change the answer.

## From a request to a brief

Instead of:

```text
Summarize this.
```

Try:

```text
Summarize the notes below for a manager who was not at the meeting.
Separate decisions, action items with owners, and open questions.
Use only information from the notes. Mark missing owners as "unassigned".

[NOTES]
```

The second version gives ChatGPT fewer decisions to invent on your behalf.

## When information is missing

You do not need to anticipate every question. Add:

```text
If essential information is missing, ask me up to three questions before
preparing the result.
```
