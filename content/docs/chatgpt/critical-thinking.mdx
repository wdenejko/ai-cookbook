title: Think critically
description: Separate facts from interpretation, reveal missing information, and verify the claims that matter.
---

A fluent answer can still be incomplete or wrong. Review the answer in layers
instead of asking only whether it “sounds good”.

## Separate three things

- **Facts from the source material** — information you actually provided or can
  verify.
- **Interpretation** — conclusions, recommendations, and explanations built
  from those facts.
- **Missing information** — details that would materially change the result.

Ask:

```text
Separate the answer into confirmed facts, interpretations, and missing
information. Mark every claim that needs an external source.
```

## Look from another side

Use a constructive devil’s advocate to expose risks, then return to the
strongest arguments in favour. Check whose perspective is missing and whether
the answer relies on a stereotype or an unconfirmed assumption.

Ready-made tools:

- [Devil’s advocate](/library/prompt/devils-advocate-pl)
- [Bias and assumptions audit](/library/prompt/bias-audit-pl)

## Verify before use

Check names, dates, numbers, quotations, legal or medical claims, and anything
that could affect another person. Prefer the original document, an official
source, or a qualified subject-matter expert.

ChatGPT can help form questions and organize evidence. It should not silently
become the person responsible for the decision.
