---
title: Work through dialogue
description: Replace the search for one perfect prompt with a short cycle of questions, drafting, review, and revision.
---

You do not need to prepare one perfect instruction before the conversation
starts. Complex work is usually easier as a sequence of small decisions.

## A calm five-step loop

1. **Set the goal** — explain what you are trying to achieve.
2. **Let ChatGPT ask** — answer only the questions that affect the result.
3. **Request a draft** — treat it as material to inspect, not a final answer.
4. **Review against criteria** — identify the single most important mismatch.
5. **Revise deliberately** — change that part while preserving what already
   works.

## Use interview mode

```text
Help me prepare [TASK]. Ask one short question at a time, no more than five in
total. Do not draft the result yet. After the final question, summarize the
brief and ask me to confirm it.
```

There is also a ready-made
[step-by-step interview prompt](/library/prompt/guided-interview-pl).

## Improve one thing at a time

Avoid asking only to “make it better”. Name the weakest part:

```text
Shorten the introduction to three sentences. Preserve all facts, the structure
of the remaining sections, and the calm tone.
```

This keeps good decisions stable and makes the effect of each revision easier
to see.

Stop when the result meets the criteria and has been checked by the person
responsible for using it. More iterations are not automatically better.
