## What ChatGPT is doing

ChatGPT creates a useful continuation from your instructions and the context
available in the conversation. This makes it good at drafting, summarizing,
reorganizing, comparing, and explaining information.

It does not automatically verify every sentence. When context is missing, it
may make a plausible guess, mix up details, or present an interpretation with
too much confidence.

## A helpful mental model

Think of a fast, well-read temporary colleague. They can prepare a strong first
draft, but they only know what you gave them and still need clear instructions
and review.

## Choose safe material

For your first tasks, use neutral or anonymized content. Do not paste:

- passwords, access tokens, or authentication details;
- personal, medical, financial, or employment records;
- confidential company documents or client information;
- material your organization has not approved for an AI tool.

When in doubt, replace names and sensitive details with placeholders.

## Try one small comparison

First ask:

```text
Write an email about a change in working hours.
```

Then add the missing decisions:

```text
Write a calm email to employees in different departments about a change in
working hours. Use no more than 150 words and finish with the three most
important details in bullets. If essential information is missing, ask me
questions before drafting.
```

Compare the two answers. Notice how much less ChatGPT needs to guess in the
second version.
