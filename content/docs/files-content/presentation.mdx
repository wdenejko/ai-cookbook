## Step by step

1. Attach only the source materials you are allowed to use.
2. Give the assistant a presentation brief:

```text
Create a plan for an 8-minute presentation to department managers. The goal is
to decide whether to run a three-month pilot. Use only the attached sources.
For each slide give: one-sentence message, evidence, suggested visual, and
speaker note. Maximum eight slides plus sources.
```

3. Review the storyline before asking for full slide text. Remove repeated ideas.
4. Ask the assistant to mark unsupported claims and add a source line to every
   slide with factual content.
5. Request a deck file only after the plan is correct, or copy the approved plan
   into PowerPoint, Google Slides, or Keynote.
6. Replace generic AI decoration with a chart, photo, or diagram that supports
   the slide's message.
7. Present once with a timer. Shorten speaker notes rather than shrinking text.

## Final review

- Can the title of each slide stand alone as its conclusion?
- Are charts labelled with units, period, and source?
- Is body text readable from the back of a room?
- Do numbers match the original materials?
- Does the final slide ask for a clear next step?

## Finished when

- [ ] The deck fits the allotted time.
- [ ] Every factual claim has a checked source.
- [ ] The audience can identify the requested decision.
