## Step by step

1. Attach the `.xlsx` or `.csv` file.
2. Ask for a data inventory:

```text
Do not calculate yet. List every sheet and column, explain the likely meaning
and unit, count empty values, and identify dates, currencies, duplicates, and
possible data-quality problems. Ask me about anything ambiguous.
```

3. Correct the interpretation of columns and filters.
4. Ask one focused question:

```text
Using only the confirmed definitions, calculate monthly sales totals and the
month-to-month percentage change. Exclude cancelled orders. Show the formula,
the number of included rows, and a reconciliation to the source total.
```

5. Request a simple chart only after the totals are correct.
6. Download any generated workbook under a new name and inspect formulas,
   filters, number formats, and chart labels in Excel.

## Verification

Manually calculate one small sample, such as one month or ten rows. Confirm that
percentages use the correct denominator and that blank values were not silently
treated as zero.

## Common traps

- Dates interpreted in US rather than European order.
- Decimal commas read as text.
- Totals duplicated by merged rows or repeated headers.
- A visually convincing chart with the wrong filter.

## Finished when

- [ ] Column meanings and exclusions are explicit.
- [ ] Source and result totals reconcile.
- [ ] One sample calculation was checked manually.
