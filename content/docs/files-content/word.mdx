## Step by step

1. Attach the `.docx` file to a new conversation.
2. Describe the reader and the single most important improvement.
3. Ask for a change list before requesting a rewritten file:

```text
Review this document for a customer who does not know our internal terminology.
First list the five changes you recommend. Do not alter names, dates, prices,
legal meaning, or commitments. Mark anything ambiguous instead of guessing.
```

4. Approve or correct the plan, then continue:

```text
Prepare a revised version using the approved changes. Keep the headings and
bullet structure. After the document, list every sentence where meaning may
have changed.
```

5. Download the result if the assistant provides a file. Open it in Word and use
   **Compare** or review it side by side with the original.
6. Check headings, tables, footnotes, dates, currencies, names, and links.

## Translation

For translation, state the target country and what must remain untranslated.
Never assume that an AI translation is suitable for a contract, medical
instruction, or certified document.

```text
Translate into Polish for a general business reader. Keep product names and
links unchanged. Put uncertain specialist terms in a final review list.
```

## Finished when

- [ ] The original file remains untouched.
- [ ] Important facts and commitments were compared.
- [ ] The final document opens correctly in Word.
