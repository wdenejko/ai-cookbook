## Install the official app

1. Open **Google Play** on Android or **App Store** on iPhone.
2. Search for **ChatGPT**, **Claude**, or **Google Gemini**.
3. Open the result and check the publisher before installing:
   - ChatGPT — OpenAI;
   - Claude — Anthropic;
   - Gemini — Google.
4. Install one assistant first. You can add another later for comparison.
5. Sign in with the same account you use on the official website.

## Decide on permissions

- **Microphone:** needed for voice conversations, not text chat.
- **Camera and photos:** needed only when you want to show or upload something.
- **Notifications:** useful for completed work and reminders; optional otherwise.
- **Contacts or location:** decline unless a feature clearly needs them.

## Run one test

```text
Ask me three questions and then create a short packing checklist for a
two-day trip. Do not assume the weather; ask for the place and date.
```

On a shared phone, sign out after use. Protect your account with the security
options offered by the provider and by your Apple or Google account.

## Finished when

- [ ] The publisher is verified.
- [ ] Only useful permissions are enabled.
- [ ] You can find a previous conversation on both phone and web.
