<SafetyNote>
Privacy settings reduce risk; they do not turn a consumer AI account into an
approved place for secrets. Follow your employer's rules and anonymize personal
information whenever possible.
</SafetyNote>

## ChatGPT

1. Open your profile menu, then **Settings** and **Data Controls**.
2. Review whether conversations may be used to improve models.
3. Open **Personalization** to inspect saved memories and chat-history references.
4. For a one-off sensitive topic, start a **Temporary Chat** and still remove identifying details.

## Claude

1. Open your profile and account settings.
2. Review privacy controls, conversation history, and Anthropic's current
   consumer data-use choices.
3. Delete conversations you no longer need. For confidential work, use only an
   organization-approved Claude plan and policy.

## Gemini

1. Open your profile, then **Gemini Apps Activity**.
2. Review whether activity is saved and choose an auto-delete period if available.
3. Inspect saved information and personalization separately.
4. Use a temporary conversation or turn activity off for a one-off task when
   the option is available, while still avoiding secrets.

## Test your choices

Start a harmless conversation, close it, then check whether it appears in
history and whether any memory or activity was saved. Settings and plan behavior
can change, so revisit this review every few months.

Use this prompt to check what the assistant claims to remember, then verify the
answer in settings rather than trusting the response alone:

```text
What personal information and preferences from previous conversations can you
currently use? List them briefly. Do not infer or add new information.
```

Official references: [OpenAI privacy controls](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893),
[Anthropic Privacy Center](https://privacy.anthropic.com/), and
[Gemini Apps Privacy Hub](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961).

## Finished when

- [ ] You know where each service stores conversations.
- [ ] Model-improvement and personalization choices match your preference.
- [ ] You can start a temporary conversation and delete an ordinary one.
