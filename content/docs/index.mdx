Welcome to **AI Cookbook** — a personal, reusable library of AI assets (ChatGPT
skills, subagents, prompts, MCP and hook configs, memories) that also doubles as
an adaptive workshop platform for a mixed audience.

This page is rendered from MDX through **Fumadocs**, styled with the
**Broadsheet** design system: Source Serif 4 on paper white, with cyan and
magenta process accents used small like spot color.

## Where this is

The foundation (Phase 0) is in place: **Next.js 16**, **Tailwind v4**, and
**Fumadocs**, themed with Broadsheet tokens. Next we build the asset library and
the workshop layer on top of this same content pipeline.
