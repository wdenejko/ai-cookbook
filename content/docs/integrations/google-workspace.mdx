## Connect

1. In ChatGPT, open your profile, **Settings**, then **Apps**.
2. Find **Gmail** or **Google Drive** and select **Connect**.
3. On Google's sign-in screen, choose the intended account. Check the full email address.
4. Read every requested permission. If the access is broader than you accept,
   cancel and ask your workspace administrator.
5. Complete the authorization and return to ChatGPT. A synchronized connection
   may need time before all permitted content is searchable.

## Run a narrow test

For Gmail:

```text
Search Gmail for the message from [known sender] with subject [exact subject].
Show its date and a three-bullet summary. Do not draft, send, label, archive, or
delete anything.
```

For Drive:

```text
Find the Google Drive file named [exact filename]. Tell me its owner, modified
date, and main headings. Do not change or share the file.
```

Compare the result with Google directly. If more than one account is connected,
name the account or workspace in the prompt.

## Disconnect

Remove the app in ChatGPT **Settings → Apps**, then review third-party access in
your Google Account security settings. OpenAI states that an indexed copy from
a disconnected Google app is deleted within 30 days.

See [Google app data controls](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10408842-google-app-for-chatgpt-data-controls-faq).

## Finished when

- [ ] The correct Google identity is connected.
- [ ] You understand the allowed read and write scopes.
- [ ] A known email or file was found without modification.
