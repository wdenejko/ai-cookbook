## Step by step

1. Choose **Deep research** from ChatGPT's tools menu.
2. Write a brief that can guide a researcher:

```text
Prepare a decision memo comparing three Polish online accounting services for
a five-person consulting company. Use information current on 26 July 2026.
Compare total price including VAT, invoice limits, bank integrations, support,
data export, cancellation, and GDPR information. Prioritize provider terms,
pricing pages, and help centers. Exclude affiliate rankings. End with a
comparison table, unresolved questions, and a recommendation with conditions.
```

3. Review the proposed plan. Add or remove sites and confirm whether uploaded or
   connected sources may be used.
4. Let the research run, but interrupt if it follows the wrong market, date, or definition.
5. In the report, open the sources behind prices, limitations, and the final recommendation.
6. Ask for a correction when a citation does not support the sentence.
7. Download the checked report in the format you need and retain its source list.

## Quality test

Ask: “Which conclusion would change if its strongest source were wrong?” Then
inspect that source first. Treat missing evidence as an unresolved question,
not permission to guess.

See the [official Deep Research guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10500283-deep-research-faq).

## Finished when

- [ ] The question includes a decision, scope, and date.
- [ ] Primary sources support high-impact claims.
- [ ] Limitations and unresolved questions are included.
