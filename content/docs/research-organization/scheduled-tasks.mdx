## Create a harmless first task

On ChatGPT web, ask:

```text
Every Monday at 08:30 Europe/Warsaw, remind me to review my priorities. Send a
short notification containing these three questions:
1. What must be finished this week?
2. What can be postponed?
3. Who needs an update?
Do not search the web or use connected apps.
```

1. Read the confirmation carefully: title, instructions, timezone, and next run.
2. Enable browser, push, or email notifications only where you want to receive them.
3. Open the task list or Scheduled page and confirm that the task appears.
4. After the first run, decide whether the output is useful. Edit, pause, or delete it.

## A recurring briefing

Once the reminder works, try a read-only briefing. Name allowed sources and ask
for “no update” when nothing material changed. Avoid vague commands such as
“watch everything” or “handle my inbox”.

Scheduled-task availability and limits vary by plan and surface. Manage tasks
from ChatGPT web when another app does not show the controls. See the
[official Tasks guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10291617-tasks-inchatgpt).

## Finished when

- [ ] Schedule and timezone are explicit.
- [ ] The first run caused no external action.
- [ ] You know where to edit, pause, and delete the task.
