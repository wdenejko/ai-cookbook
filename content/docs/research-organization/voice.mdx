## Start voice

1. Open ChatGPT on the web or in the mobile app.
2. Select the **Voice** icon in the message area.
3. Allow microphone access when prompted. Camera and screen access are separate
   and should stay off unless you explicitly need them.
4. Begin with a working agreement:

```text
I want to think through a plan. Ask one question at a time. If I pause, wait
until I say "your turn". At the end, create a written summary with decisions,
open questions, and next actions.
```

5. Speak in short sections. Correct misheard names, dates, and numbers immediately.
6. End voice mode, read the transcript, and ask for the promised written summary.

## Useful voice tasks

- Rehearse a presentation while the assistant asks audience questions.
- Turn a walk-and-talk brainstorm into an organized list.
- Practice a foreign-language conversation at a chosen level.
- Dictate a rough message, then edit the text before sending.

Voice availability and options depend on plan, region, and app version. See the
[current ChatGPT Voice guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001274/).

## Finished when

- [ ] You can mute and end the conversation quickly.
- [ ] Important details were checked in the transcript.
- [ ] The useful result exists as editable text.
