### Heading three — 25px

Body text is set in Source Serif 4 at a comfortable measure. Emphasis takes the
serif's *true italic voice*, and [links use the cyan accent](/docs). The two
process accents — cyan (`--color-accent`) and magenta (`--color-accent-2`) —
each carry a 100–900 tonal ramp generated on a shared perceptual lightness
scale.

## Spacing & rhythm

Spacing follows Broadsheet's airy 1.25× density (a 5px base), expressed in `rem`
so the text-size and comfort controls scale the whole interface — not just the
copy.
