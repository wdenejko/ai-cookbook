---
title: Participant checklist
description: A one-page summary to use during and after the workshop.
---

## Before sending a prompt

- What is my goal?
- What context is necessary?
- Which source data should the model use?
- Who is the audience?
- What should the result look like?
- How will I recognize a good answer?
- Does the material contain confidential or personal data?

## When you cannot formulate the prompt

> Ask me no more than five questions, one at a time. Then summarize the brief
> and ask me to confirm it before completing the task.

## Before using the answer

- Which statements come from my source material?
- Which statements are the model’s interpretation?
- What requires an external source or a subject-matter expert?
- Which perspectives or affected groups are missing?
- Is the tone appropriate for the audience?

## For images

- Confirm the purpose, format, composition, style, and exclusions.
- Edit one variable at a time.
- Check text, hands, faces, logos, private details, and stereotypes.

## Choose the right place

- **New chat:** a separate topic or clean context.
- **Project:** related chats and files for one initiative.
- **Memory:** stable personal preferences worth reusing.
- **Skill:** a repeated process with clear steps and quality criteria.

Never paste passwords, tokens, personal records, or confidential company
material into a tool unless your organization has explicitly approved it.
