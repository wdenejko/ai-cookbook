---
title: Prompt cards
description: Short, ready-to-use instructions for demonstrations, exercises, and participant handouts.
---

## 1. Complete a brief

```text
My goal is:
[GOAL]

Context:
[CONTEXT]

Audience:
[AUDIENCE]

Expected format:
[FORMAT]

Quality criteria:
[CRITERIA]

If essential information is missing, ask questions before completing the task.
```

## 2. Interview me step by step

```text
Help me prepare [TASK]. Ask one short question at a time, no more than five in
total. Do not draft the result yet. After the final question, summarize the
brief and ask me to confirm it.
```

## 3. Constructive devil’s advocate

```text
Review this decision: [DECISION].
First present the strongest arguments against it, then the strongest arguments
for it. List risks, assumptions, and missing information. Do not invent facts.
Finish with questions I should answer before deciding.
```

## 4. Bias and assumptions audit

```text
Review the answer below. Separate facts from interpretation, identify hidden
assumptions, missing perspectives, and possible stereotypes. Mark claims that
need verification and propose a more balanced version.

[ANSWER]
```

## 5. Improve a draft

```text
Improve only [ONE ASPECT] of the draft below. Preserve its meaning, facts, and
all other choices. Explain briefly what you changed and return the revised
version.

[DRAFT]
```

## 6. Visual brief

```text
Create a [FORMAT] image for [PURPOSE].
Main subject: [SUBJECT].
Setting and composition: [SETTING / PLACEMENT].
Style, colour, and lighting: [STYLE].
Leave free space for: [TEXT OR UI].
Do not include: [EXCLUSIONS].
```

Replace bracketed fields before use. Treat model output as a draft and verify
important facts against reliable sources.
