---
title: ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini?
description: Choose an AI assistant for the task instead of looking for one permanent winner.
---

ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini can all write, explain, analyze files and search for
information. The useful question is not “Which brand is best?” but “Which
product fits this task, my tools and my data rules?”

<LessonGuide slug="choosing-an-ai-assistant" locale="en" />

<Concept title="Start with the job, not the brand">

Name the result you need before opening an app. A quick answer, a careful
document review, a spreadsheet analysis and work inside Gmail are four different
jobs. Also note whether you need current web sources, image creation, voice,
collaboration or a reusable project.

The best choice is the product that completes the whole task with the least
copying between tools and enough control over sources and data.

</Concept>

<Concept title="Use a practical starting point">

- **ChatGPT** is a strong general starting point when one assistant should cover
  varied work: web search, files, spreadsheet analysis and charts, image
  generation, voice and longer-running Projects. See the official
  [ChatGPT overview](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12677804-what-is-chatgpt-faq)
  and [data analysis guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8437071-data-analysis-with-chatgpt/).
- **Claude** is worth testing for document-heavy work and outputs that you want
  to develop separately from the chat. Its Projects hold source material, while
  Artifacts provide a dedicated space for documents, diagrams and interactive
  results. See [Claude Projects](https://support.anthropic.com/en/articles/9517075-what-are-projects)
  and [Artifacts](https://support.anthropic.com/en/articles/9487310-what-are-artifacts-and-how-do-i-use-them).
- **Gemini** is a natural candidate when the work already lives in Google
  services. Depending on the account, it can use Gmail, Drive, Calendar and
  other connected apps, while Deep Research uses Google Search and selected
  sources. See [Connected Apps](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14959807)
  and [Gemini Deep Research](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15719111).

These are starting points, not permanent rankings.

</Concept>

<Concept title="Run the same test before paying">

Choose three real tasks and run them in every product with the same input:

1. rewrite a short message for a specific audience;
2. answer questions about the same document and cite the relevant pages;
3. research a current topic and provide checkable sources.

Score usefulness, Polish language quality, source handling, ease of revision,
speed, limits and privacy settings. A comparison based on your work is more
valuable than a general benchmark.

</Concept>

<Concept title="Expect features to move">

Plans, limits, model names and integrations change frequently. They can also
differ by country, device, personal or work account and administrator settings.
Check the product interface and official help before buying a subscription or
teaching a feature to a team.

This comparison was reviewed in **July 2026**. The lesson deliberately avoids
fixed prices and message limits so that its decision method remains useful.

</Concept>

<GoDeeper title="Use one neutral scorecard">

Give every product the same criteria and weights: result quality, evidence,
workflow fit, privacy, accessibility, cost and time saved. Keep the original
outputs. If one tool wins only because it received a better prompt or a different
file, the comparison is not fair.

</GoDeeper>
