---
title: Protect your data when using AI
description: Decide what can be shared, remove unnecessary details and check the account settings before uploading anything sensitive.
---

An AI assistant is an external service, not a private notebook. Before pasting
text or attaching a file, decide whether the service is allowed to receive that
information and whether the task can be completed with less data.

<LessonGuide slug="privacy-and-safe-ai-use" locale="en" />

<Concept title="Recognize sensitive information">

Stop before sharing passwords, access codes, payment details, identity
documents, medical records, confidential company material or information about
another person. A file can also reveal more than its visible paragraphs through
comments, tracked changes, hidden sheets, filenames and metadata.

A useful rule is: if you would not send the material to an unknown external
consultant, do not send it to a consumer AI account without approval.

</Concept>

<Concept title="Share the minimum">

Remove everything the task does not require. Replace names with roles, exact
amounts with ranges and customer identifiers with neutral labels. Extract the
relevant page instead of uploading an entire folder. When possible, create a
small fictional example that preserves the structure of the problem.

Anonymization is not merely deleting a name. A rare job title, address, date and
event can identify someone when combined.

</Concept>

<Concept title="Understand the account and its controls">

Personal and organization-managed accounts can have different retention,
training and connector rules. In ChatGPT, Data Controls can disable the use of
new consumer conversations for model improvement, while Temporary Chats do not
create history or memories and are deleted after 30 days. Review the current
[ChatGPT Data Controls](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-chatgpt-data-usage-for-model-training).

Other products use different controls. For example, Gemini documents how Keep
Activity, temporary chats, human review and connected apps affect data handling
in its [Privacy Hub](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961).

</Concept>

<Concept title="Connected apps widen the boundary">

Connecting mail, cloud storage or a calendar can save time, but it also gives
the assistant another source of context. Check which account is connected, what
the product may retrieve and how to disconnect it. Never assume that deleting a
chat also deletes information retained by a connected service.

At work, follow the organization’s approved tools and classification rules.
Changing a consumer setting does not replace company permission.

</Concept>

<GoDeeper title="Use a three-question sharing check">

Before sending material, ask:

1. **May I share it?** Consider ownership, consent and workplace rules.
2. **Does the task need it?** Remove unnecessary fields and pages.
3. **Can I verify what happens next?** Check the account, settings, retention
   and connected apps.

If any answer is unclear, pause and use a sanitized example.

</GoDeeper>
