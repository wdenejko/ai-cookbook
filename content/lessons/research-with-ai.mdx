---
title: Research with AI and verify the sources
description: Move from a clear question through a research plan and reliable sources to a conclusion you can check.
---

AI research is more than asking a model what it knows. A useful process searches
current material, distinguishes evidence from interpretation and leaves a
visible path from every important claim back to its source.

<LessonGuide slug="research-with-ai" locale="en" />

<Concept title="Choose chat, search or deep research">

Use ordinary chat to shape a question or understand a stable concept. Use web
search when the answer depends on current facts. Use a deep-research mode when
the question needs several searches, comparison across sources and a structured
report.

ChatGPT offers [web search](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-chatgpt-search),
Claude offers [Research](https://support.anthropic.com/en/articles/11088861-using-research-on-claude-ai),
and Gemini offers [Deep Research](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15719111).
Availability and limits depend on the plan and account.

</Concept>

<Concept title="Approve the plan before the report">

Define the main question, timeframe, geography and exclusions. Ask the assistant
to propose subquestions and a source plan before it begins. Remove irrelevant
branches and add the evidence needed to make a decision.

A ten-page report answering the wrong question is still a failed research task.

</Concept>

<Concept title="Prefer the closest reliable source">

For a law, use the official legal text or government guidance. For a product
feature, use the manufacturer’s current documentation. For research findings,
look for the original paper or dataset. News and commentary can provide context,
but they should not silently replace the primary evidence.

Check both the publication date and the date when the described event happened.

</Concept>

<Concept title="Open the citations">

A citation proves only that a page exists. Open it and check whether it supports
the exact sentence, whether the quotation is accurate and whether important
conditions were omitted. Mark claims that are based on one source, disputed or
inferred by the assistant.

Finish with three lists: confirmed findings, unresolved questions and decisions
that still require a person.

</Concept>

<GoDeeper title="Research by confidence, not by volume">

Ask for a claim table containing the claim, source, source type, date, supporting
passage and confidence. Investigate the high-impact claims with weak or
conflicting support first. Adding ten similar articles does not create ten
independent pieces of evidence if they all repeat the same original story.

</GoDeeper>
