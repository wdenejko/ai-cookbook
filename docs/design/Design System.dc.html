03

Patterns

Whitespace over rules

Sections are separated by space and the serif scale — no boxes, no dividers. The card is the only boxed component, used for discrete listings.

Cyan first, magenta rare

Cyan carries every interactive element; magenta is the second spot color for warnings and favorites — never both in one small component.

Ink mode

Dark mode inverts the press: near-black ground, paper-white ink, the same two spot colors nudged lighter for contrast. Toggle it in the masthead.

Adaptive & accessible

Level switcher, glossary popovers on focus, 44px targets and a bilingual toggle — one artifact that serves IT pros, teenagers, office staff and seniors.
